# Unpopular Tourist Places

**Archived.** A travel site for lesser-known destinations — places, things to
do, and nearby hotels. School project, **November 2023**.

Three static pages (`index.html`, `things_to_do.html`, `hotels.html`) with one
shared stylesheet and one script, plus a banner video and about 30 MB of
photographs.

---

## Why it is kept

Early work, kept honestly rather than deleted. It sits between
[`Cyber-Safety`](https://github.com/DDXPlayz/Cyber-Safety) (2022) and
[`Olympics2024`](https://github.com/DDXPlayz/Olympics2024) (2024), and the
three together show a year-by-year progression that neither one shows alone.

The photographs are the property of their respective owners and were used for a
non-commercial student project.
